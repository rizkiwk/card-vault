## Full description (≤ 4000 chars)

```
CardVault is a fast, private, fully offline app to catalog and value your
trading card collection — no account, no ads, no internet required.

Whether you collect Pokémon, One Piece, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Magic: The Gathering, or
sports cards, CardVault keeps your whole collection organized in one place and
100% on your device.

★ KEY FEATURES

• Add cards in seconds — capture a photo with your camera or pick one from your
  gallery, then fill in name, set, number, rarity, condition and quantity.
• Track value — record purchase price and current value, and see your total
  collection worth plus gain/loss at a glance.
• Powerful organization — group by game, tag cards your own way, mark favorites,
  and keep a separate wishlist for the cards you're still chasing.
• Fast search & filters — find any card by name, set, number or notes, and
  filter by game, condition, tags or value range.
• Statistics dashboard — visual breakdowns of value by game, rarity and
  condition, plus your top most-valuable cards.
• Backup & restore — export your entire collection (data + images) to a single
  file and import it back anytime. Your data, your control.
• Light & dark themes, multiple currencies.

★ PRIVATE BY DESIGN

CardVault works entirely offline. There are no user accounts and no servers —
your cards, photos and prices never leave your device. We don't collect or share
any personal data.

★ WHAT CARDVAULT IS NOT

No marketplace, no buying or selling, no payments, no chat. Just a clean, focused
vault for collectors who want to know exactly what they own and what it's worth.

★ PERMISSIONS

• Camera — only to photograph your own cards (requested when you take a photo).
• Photos — only to attach images you choose from your gallery.

——

CardVault is an independent, unofficial app for personal collection management.
It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by The Pokémon Company,
Bandai/Toei (One Piece), Konami (Yu-Gi-Oh!), Wizards of the Coast (Magic: The
Gathering), or any sports league or card manufacturer. All product names,
logos and brands are property of their respective owners and are used only to
describe collection compatibility.
```

## Keywords / ASO notes
Natural-language phrases already woven into the copy (Play indexes the full
description — avoid keyword stuffing, which violates policy):
`card collection tracker`, `TCG collection`, `offline card manager`,
`trading card organizer`, `collection value tracker`, `wishlist`, `card binder`.

Avoid trademarked terms in the **app name** and **icon**. Using them once,
nominatively, in the description body is acceptable.
