# Privacy Policy — CardVault

**Last updated:** 5 June 2026
**Effective date:** 5 June 2026

CardVault ("the app", "we", "us") is an offline collection-management app
published for personal use. This policy explains what data the app handles and,
more importantly, what it does **not**.

> **Summary:** CardVault works entirely on your device. We do not collect,
> transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no user accounts and no
> servers.

---

## 1. Data we collect

**None.** CardVault has no servers and no analytics. We do not collect personal
information, usage data, advertising identifiers, or location.

## 2. Data you create (stored only on your device)

When you use the app you may enter or capture:

* Card details (name, set, number, rarity, condition, quantity, prices, notes).
* Tags, favorites and wishlist entries.
* Photos of your cards that you take with the camera or select from your gallery.

All of this is stored **locally** on your device in the app's private storage
and a local database. It is never uploaded to us or to any third party.

## 3. Permissions and why they are used

| Permission | Purpose | When requested |
|-----------|---------|----------------|
| Camera | Take photos of your own cards to attach to entries | Only when you tap "Camera" |
| Photos / Media | Attach an image you choose from your gallery | Only when you tap "Gallery" |

Images you add are copied into the app's private storage so they appear in your
collection. They are not shared with anyone.

## 4. Backups and exports

The app lets you export your collection (data and images) to a file you choose,
and import it back later. These backup files are created at **your** request and
stored where **you** place them (for example, your device storage or a location
you pick when sharing). We never receive a copy. You are responsible for the
security of any backup files you create or share.

## 5. Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose your data, because we never have access to it.
The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can remove individual
cards, or use **Settings → Clear all data** to permanently erase the entire
collection. Uninstalling the app also removes all app data from your device.

## 7. Children's privacy

CardVault does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone,
including children under 13. The app collects no data and requires no account.

## 8. Security

Because your data never leaves your device, there is no transmission to secure.
On-device data is protected by your device's own security (lock screen,
encryption). We recommend keeping your device and any backup files protected.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised and the
updated policy will be published at the same URL referenced in the app's store
listing.
