CardVault

Privacy Policy

Your collection data is yours alone — private, local, and always under your control.

100% Offline · No data collection

Short version: CardVault stores everything exclusively on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no backend, no analytics, and no account system.

Effective date: June 5, 2026  ·  App version: 1.0

📋 1. Information We Collect

We collect nothing. CardVault does not collect, process, or transmit any personal information. The app operates fully offline and has no internet permission in its Android manifest.

All data you enter — card names, prices, photos, notes, tags — is stored exclusively in a local SQLite database and the private file directory on your device.

📱 2. Data Stored on Your Device

CardVault stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Card details (name, game, set, condition, rarity, value, quantity)
  • Card photos you capture or import from your gallery
  • Tags, wishlists, and notes you create
  • App preferences (currency, theme)

This data is stored in your device's private app storage and is not accessible to other apps or to us.

📷 3. Camera & Photo Permissions

CardVault requests the following permissions solely to let you photograph your cards:

  • CAMERA — to take photos of cards directly in the app
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES — to import existing photos from your gallery

Photos are compressed and saved to your device's private app directory. They are never uploaded to any server.

🌐 4. Internet Access

CardVault does not declare the INTERNET permission. The app is physically incapable of making any network requests. No data can leave your device through the app.

💾 5. Backup & Export

CardVault includes an optional backup feature that packages your collection into a .cardvault file (a zip archive of your database and card images). This file is created and stored locally or shared to a destination you choose (e.g. your cloud storage app, email).

We never receive or have access to this backup file. You are in full control of where it goes.

🔒 6. Third-Party Services

CardVault does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, analytics, advertising networks, or crash reporting tools. There are no trackers of any kind in the app.

👶 7. Children's Privacy

CardVault does not collect any personal data from any user, including children under 13. The app is safe for all ages from a data privacy standpoint.

🔄 8. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the effective date above and publish the revised policy at this URL. Continued use of the app after any update constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
